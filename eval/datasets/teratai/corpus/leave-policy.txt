# Annual Leave

Every permanent employee of Teratai Digital Sdn Bhd receives annual leave based on length of service. Staff with less than two years of service receive 14 working days per calendar year. Staff with two to five years receive 18 days, and staff with more than five years receive 22 days. Leave is credited on 1 January and pro-rated for employees who join or leave during the year. Annual leave must be applied for through the HRPortal at least five working days in advance and approved by the reporting manager before it is taken.

# Medical Leave

Employees who are unwell are entitled to paid medical leave of up to 14 days per year, or 60 days when hospitalised. A medical certificate (MC) from a registered medical practitioner must be uploaded to HRPortal within 48 hours of returning to work. Medical leave taken without a valid certificate is converted to unpaid leave. Employees must inform their manager before 10:00 am on the first day of absence.

# Maternity and Paternity Leave

Female employees are entitled to 98 consecutive days of paid maternity leave for each confinement. Male employees are entitled to 7 consecutive days of paid paternity leave, which must be taken within 30 days of the child's birth. Employees should notify Human Resources at least 30 days before the expected date of delivery so that cover can be arranged.

# Emergency Leave

Up to 3 days of emergency leave per year may be taken for sudden, unforeseen events such as a fire or flood at home, or the serious illness of an immediate family member. Emergency leave may be applied for retrospectively, within two working days of returning. Compassionate leave of 3 days is granted separately on the death of a spouse, child, parent or sibling.

# Unpaid Leave

Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be approved by the Head of Department once all annual leave entitlement has been used. Unpaid leave beyond 30 days requires approval from the Chief People Officer. Benefits such as medical insurance continue during unpaid leave, but EPF contributions are not made for unpaid days.

# Carry Forward

A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the following year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March, after which they lapse. Unused leave is not paid out in cash except on resignation, when outstanding days are paid at the daily basic salary rate.

# Public Holidays

The company observes the national public holidays gazetted for Malaysia together with the state holidays of Selangor. When a public holiday falls on a Saturday, the following Monday is not a replacement day; when it falls on a Sunday, the following Monday is a replacement holiday. The holiday calendar for the year is published on HRPortal every December.
