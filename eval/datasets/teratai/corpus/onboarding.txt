# Before Your First Day

New employees receive a welcome email from Human Resources one week before their start date, with the reporting time, office address and documents to bring: identity card, bank account details, EPF number and educational certificates. Managers must submit an equipment request to IT at least five working days before the start date.

# Your First Day

On the first day, new joiners report to reception at 9:00 am on Level 12, where they collect their access badge and laptop. The morning is spent on an HR orientation session, followed by lunch with the team and an introduction to their onboarding buddy.

# Probation Period

Executives serve a probation period of three months, and managers and above serve six months. A probation review is held two weeks before the end of the period. Probation may be extended once by up to three months if expectations have not yet been met. Notice period during probation is two weeks.

# Mandatory Training

All new employees must complete the mandatory e-learning modules within 30 days of joining: Code of Conduct, Information Security Awareness, Anti-Bribery and Corruption, and Personal Data Protection. Completion is tracked in the LearnHub platform and reported to managers.

# Buddy Programme

Every new joiner is paired with a buddy from a different team for the first 90 days. Buddies answer day-to-day questions, explain informal ways of working and meet the new joiner at least once every two weeks. Buddies are volunteers and receive recognition points for their support.

# Payroll and Statutory Registration

Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month by bank transfer. New joiners must submit their EPF, SOCSO and EIS details and income tax number to Payroll during the first week so that statutory deductions can be made correctly. Joiners who start after the 15th receive their first salary in the following month.
