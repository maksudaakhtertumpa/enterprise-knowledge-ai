# Booking Business Travel

All flights and hotels for business travel must be booked through the company's travel desk, TravelHub, at least 14 days before departure. Economy class is used for flights under six hours. Business class may be approved by a Director for flights longer than six hours. Hotel bookings should not exceed RM 350 per night within Malaysia.

# Daily Allowance

Employees travelling on company business receive a daily meal allowance of RM 60 within Peninsular Malaysia and RM 80 in Sabah and Sarawak. The allowance covers breakfast, lunch and dinner and is paid per full day of travel. Half the allowance is paid for travel days that start after 12:00 noon or end before 12:00 noon. No allowance is paid when meals are provided by a client or at a conference.

# Mileage Claims

Staff who use their own car for business trips may claim mileage at 60 sen per kilometre for the first 500 km in a month and 50 sen per kilometre thereafter. Toll and parking charges may be claimed separately with receipts. Travel between home and the office is not claimable.

# Submitting Expense Claims

Expense claims are submitted using form FIN-12 in the ExpenseOne system within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Original receipts or tax invoices must be attached for every item above RM 20. Claims are approved by the line manager and then verified by Finance. Approved claims are reimbursed together with the next monthly salary payment.

# Non-Claimable Expenses

The following are not reimbursed: alcoholic drinks, traffic summonses and parking fines, personal entertainment such as in-room movies, minibar items, laundry for trips shorter than five days, and upgrades that were not pre-approved. Expenses for a spouse or family member accompanying the employee are also excluded.

# Overseas Travel

Overseas trips require approval from the Head of Department and the Chief Financial Officer. Travel insurance is arranged automatically by TravelHub. The daily allowance for overseas travel is USD 75 for Singapore, Hong Kong, Japan and Australia, and USD 50 for other countries. Employees must check the government's travel advisories before departure.
