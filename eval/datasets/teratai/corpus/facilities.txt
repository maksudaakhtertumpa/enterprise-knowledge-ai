# Office Access

The office at Menara Teratai is open from 7:00 am to 10:00 pm on weekdays. Access requires a personal access badge, which must be worn visibly at all times. Badges must not be lent to others, and tailgating through the security gates is not permitted. Weekend access requires prior approval from Facilities.

# Meeting Room Booking

Meeting rooms are booked through Outlook. Bookings are limited to two hours at a time, and rooms not occupied within 15 minutes of the start time are automatically released. The boardroom on Level 15 can only be booked by the CEO's office.

# Parking

Staff parking is available on levels B2 and B3 on a first-come, first-served basis. Parking bays with electric vehicle chargers are reserved for EVs while charging and are limited to four hours. Visitors should be registered with reception to receive a visitor parking pass.

# Emergency Evacuation

When the fire alarm sounds, leave the building immediately using the nearest staircase; do not use the lifts. Assemble at the assembly point in Car Park C and report to your floor warden for the headcount. Fire drills are held twice a year.

# Lost and Found

Items found in the office should be handed to reception, where they are logged and kept for 30 days. Unclaimed items are donated to charity after that period, and unclaimed company equipment is returned to IT.

# Pantry and Shared Spaces

Coffee, tea and drinking water are provided free in the pantries on every floor. Food left in the shared refrigerators is cleared every Friday at 5:00 pm. Employees are expected to wash their own cups and keep shared areas tidy.
