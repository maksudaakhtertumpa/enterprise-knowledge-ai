# Goal Setting

Each employee agrees three to five objectives with their manager at the start of every quarter using the company's OKR framework. Objectives are recorded in the PeopleGrow system and should be specific, measurable and linked to the team's goals.

# Mid-Year Review

A mid-year check-in is held in July to review progress against objectives, discuss development needs and adjust goals where business priorities have changed. The mid-year review does not produce a rating.

# Annual Performance Appraisal

The annual appraisal is completed in January. Employees first complete a self-assessment, followed by a review discussion with their manager. Performance is rated on a five-point scale from 1 (does not meet expectations) to 5 (exceptional). Ratings are calibrated across departments to ensure consistency.

# Performance Improvement Plan

An employee rated 1, or whose performance raises serious concerns, may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 60 days. The PIP sets out clear targets and the support provided, with fortnightly progress meetings. Failure to meet the targets may lead to termination of employment.

# Promotions

Promotions are considered once a year in April, based on the annual appraisal, readiness for the next level and business need. Employees must normally have spent at least 18 months in their current grade to be eligible. Promotions are approved by the Promotion Committee.

# Salary Increments

Annual salary increments take effect on 1 April and are linked to the appraisal rating and the employee's position in the salary band. Employees rated 1 do not receive an increment. A one-off performance bonus may also be paid in March depending on company results.
