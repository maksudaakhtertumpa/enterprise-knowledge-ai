# Medical Insurance

All permanent employees and their spouse and children are covered by the group medical insurance plan. Outpatient treatment is free at panel clinics on presentation of the medical card. Hospitalisation and surgical benefits are covered up to RM 50,000 per year for each insured person, with cashless admission at panel hospitals.

# Dental Benefits

Employees may claim up to RM 500 per year for dental treatment, including scaling, fillings and extractions. Cosmetic treatments such as whitening and braces are not covered. Claims are submitted in ExpenseOne with the original receipt.

# Optical Benefits

Employees may claim up to RM 300 every two years for an eye examination, spectacles or contact lenses. The two-year period is counted from the date of the previous claim.

# Wellness Allowance

A wellness allowance of RM 600 per year may be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, sports equipment or health screenings. The allowance is not carried forward into the next year.

# Employee Assistance Programme

The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) offers free and confidential counselling for employees and their immediate family members, covering stress, relationships, financial worries and grief. Up to six sessions per issue are provided by an external provider, and the company does not receive any information about who uses the service.

# Professional Certification Sponsorship

The company sponsors professional certifications relevant to an employee's role, such as cloud, security or accounting qualifications, covering course and examination fees. Sponsorship above RM 5,000 requires the employee to sign a bond agreement to stay with the company for 12 months after certification, or repay the fees on a pro-rated basis.
