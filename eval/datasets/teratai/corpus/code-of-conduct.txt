# Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts from customers or suppliers with a value of up to RM 200. Any gift above RM 200 must be declined, or reported in the Gift Register within five working days and handed to the Compliance team. Cash, vouchers and gift cards must never be accepted regardless of value. Hospitality such as a business meal is acceptable if it is reasonable and not offered during an active tender.

# Conflicts of Interest

A conflict of interest arises when personal interests could influence business decisions, for example owning shares in a supplier or hiring a relative. Employees must declare any actual or potential conflict to their manager and the Compliance team using the Conflict Declaration form, and must not take part in decisions affected by the conflict.

# Anti-Bribery and Corruption

Teratai Digital has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption, in line with Section 17A of the Malaysian Anti-Corruption Commission Act. Offering, giving, requesting or accepting anything of value to obtain an improper business advantage is prohibited. Facilitation payments are also prohibited. Breaches lead to dismissal and may be reported to the authorities.

# Whistleblowing

Concerns about misconduct can be raised confidentially through the independent ethics hotline at 1-800-88-7788 or the online portal at speakup.teratai.example. Reports may be made anonymously. The company prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, and investigations are overseen by the Audit Committee.

# Harassment and Discrimination

Harassment, bullying and discrimination on the basis of race, religion, gender, age, disability or any other protected characteristic are not tolerated. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by a dedicated inquiry panel and must be investigated within 30 days of being received.

# Social Media

Employees must not disclose confidential company information, client names or unreleased product details on social media. Only the Corporate Communications team may speak on behalf of the company. Personal accounts that mention employment at Teratai Digital should state that views expressed are personal.
